# IronMind Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 20, 2026

## Overview

IronMind ("we," "us," "our," or "App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our offline-first fitness tracking application handles data.

## Key Privacy Principles

IronMind is designed with privacy as a core principle:
- **100% Offline Operation**: All workout data stays on your device
- **No Account Required**: Use the app without creating an account
- **No Ads or Tracking**: We do not display ads or track your activity
- **No Third-Party Sharing**: Your fitness data is never sold or shared with third parties

## Data Collection

### What We Collect

IronMind collects and stores only the workout data you explicitly enter:
- Exercise names and types
- Sets, reps, and weights logged
- Workout sessions and timestamps
- Training routines you create
- Muscle group and equipment preferences
- Weight unit preference (kg or lbs)

### What We Do NOT Collect

- Personal identification (name, email, phone number)
- Location data
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Crash reports or diagnostics (optional, if enabled by user)

## Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device in an encrypted SQLite database. **Your data never leaves your device** unless you explicitly export it via the backup feature.

### Backup Feature

When you use the backup feature:
- Exports are stored only to locations you choose (device storage, cloud drive, etc.)
- We do not access, store, or process your backups
- You maintain complete control over backup files

## AI Features

IronMind includes an optional on-device AI coach:
- The AI model runs entirely on your device
- No data is sent to external servers
- Suggestions are generated offline using the Gemma 3 model
- The model is cached locally after first download

## Permissions

IronMind requests the following permissions:

| Permission | Purpose | Why Needed |
|-----------|---------|-----------|
| CAMERA | Take photos of weights for OCR | Optional feature to read weight markings |
| POST_NOTIFICATIONS | Rest timer alerts | Notify you when rest periods end |
| ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE | Detect connection type | Warn about large downloads on mobile data |
| READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE | Import model files | Allow importing AI models from storage |

All permissions are optional. The app functions fully without them.

## Children's Privacy

IronMind is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
