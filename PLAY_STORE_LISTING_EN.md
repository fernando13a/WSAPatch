## Full Description (4000 characters max)

**Your Personal AI Strength Coach - 100% Offline**

IronMind is an offline-first fitness tracking app that combines a powerful workout logger with an on-device AI coach to help you progress safely and efficiently.

### Key Features

**📊 Smart Workout Tracking**
- Log exercises, sets, reps, and weight instantly
- Create custom training routines (Push/Pull/Legs splits and more)
- Auto-calculate plate configurations for barbells
- Read weight from photos using on-device OCR
- Track volume, streaks, and training history

**🧠 AI Coach (On-Device)**
- Gemma 3 AI model runs entirely on your device
- Gets smarter as you log more workouts
- Analyzes your progress and suggests progressive overload
- 100% private - your data never leaves your phone
- Download once (~555 MB), works offline forever

**🔐 Privacy First**
- No account required
- No ads, no tracking, no analytics
- Your data stays on your device
- Export backups to anywhere you choose
- Plain SQLite database you can inspect

**⚡ Lightweight & Fast**
- Minimal battery drain
- Works without internet
- Instant muscle group & equipment filtering
- Bilingual (Spanish/English) with device locale auto-detection
- Rest timer with notification alerts

**🏋️ Exercises & Customization**
- 500+ exercise catalog (bilingual)
- Create unlimited routines
- Customize exercise names and descriptions
- Reference images for proper form
- Equipment and muscle group categorization

### Perfect For
- Strength athletes tracking progressive overload
- Personal trainers and coaches managing client workouts
- Anyone wanting privacy-first fitness tracking
- Users who train without internet access
- People who prefer local-first, offline-capable apps

### What's NOT Included
- No social features or workout sharing
- No macronutrient tracking
- No cardio-focused metrics
- No cloud sync or device sync

### Technical Details
- Works offline 100% of the time
- Android 8.0+ (minSdk 26, targetSdk 35)
- ~50 MB app size
- ~1-2 GB data storage for typical heavy lifting routines
- Material 3 design with dark mode

### Permissions
- Camera: Optional, for reading weight from photos
- Notifications: Optional, for rest timer alerts
- Network: Optional, only for downloading AI model
- File access: Only for backup/restore

### Open Source
IronMind's architecture follows clean principles (domain/data/UI layers) and is built with modern Android best practices. See GitHub for code and contribution guidelines.

### Get Started
1. Install IronMind free
2. Create your first routine or start a free session
3. Log your first set and get instant feedback
4. Download the AI model (~555 MB, one-time)
5. Receive personalized coaching based on your progress

**Your fitness data is yours alone. Always.**
